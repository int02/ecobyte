What We Do

Four Pillars of Green ICT & Responsible Internet Use

Digital Education Programs

Hands-on workshops, classroom modules, and community learning sessions that teach sustainable technology practices, critical digital citizenship, and eco-conscious computing from barangay halls to university campuses.

Device Recycling & E-Waste Management

Certified collection drives, partnerships with DENR-accredited recyclers, and community refurbishment hubs that keep phones, laptops, and batteries out of open dumps — recovering materials and extending device lifespans.

Energy-Efficient Technology Advocacy

Energy audits for schools and offices, ENERGY STAR device guidance, power-management toolkits, and policy recommendations that slash kilowatt-hour use and lower the carbon footprint of digital infrastructure.

Online Privacy & Data Protection

Free privacy audits, Data Privacy Act (DPA) briefings, password-safety campaigns, and guides to minimizing digital traces — because responsible internet use protects both the planet and the people on it.
