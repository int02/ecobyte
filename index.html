About EcoByte

Our mission is to make digital life cleaner, safer, and more responsible for everyone.

Founded in Davao City, EcoByte is a community-driven initiative bridging environmental sustainability and digital technology. We believe that Green ICT isn't an option — it's an essential responsibility for every person, school, and business in a connected world.

Our Story & Identity

EcoByte began as a student-led campaign to address the growing environmental and social costs of unregulated digital consumption. Today, we work with schools, barangays, and local governments across Mindanao to design programs that turn everyday technology users into informed stewards of the digital planet.

Read More
Our Mission

To empower Filipino communities with the knowledge, tools, and support systems needed to adopt energy-efficient technology, eliminate toxic e-waste, practice responsible internet use, and protect digital privacy — building a circular, inclusive, and low-carbon digital economy one device at a time.

Read More
Our Vision 2030

A Philippines where every household uses energy-saving devices, every community has a certified e-waste collection point, every child learns digital citizenship in school, and every citizen navigates the internet with awareness — creating a zero-waste digital nation that leads ASEAN in Green ICT innovation.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Green ICT & Responsible Internet Use

Digital Education Programs

Hands-on workshops, classroom modules, and community learning sessions that teach sustainable technology practices, critical digital citizenship, and eco-conscious computing from barangay halls to university campuses.

Device Recycling & E-Waste Management

Certified collection drives, partnerships with DENR-accredited recyclers, and community refurbishment hubs that keep phones, laptops, and batteries out of open dumps — recovering materials and extending device lifespans.

Energy-Efficient Technology Advocacy

Energy audits for schools and offices, ENERGY STAR device guidance, power-management toolkits, and policy recommendations that slash kilowatt-hour use and lower the carbon footprint of digital infrastructure.

Online Privacy & Data Protection

Free privacy audits, Data Privacy Act (DPA) briefings, password-safety campaigns, and guides to minimizing digital traces — because responsible internet use protects both the planet and the people on it.

Volunteer with EcoByte

Lend Your Skills to a Greener, Safer Digital Philippines.

Whether you're a college student who can teach basic computing, an IT professional who can refurbish laptops on weekends, or a concerned parent who wants to run a barangay privacy workshop — there's a place for you on the EcoByte team. No tech degree required, just heart and curiosity.

12 flexible volunteer tracks, from 2-hour event shifts to 6-month program leadership roles.

Every new volunteer receives a full EcoByte Green ICT Training Kit, DSWD community-service hours, and a letter of recommendation upon completion.

Work alongside youth leaders, barangay officials, DENR staff, and IT mentors from the Davao tech community.

Hybrid roles available — some volunteers work fully online curating educational content or moderating our community forums.

100% free to join. We never ask volunteers for donations; we ask for your time, ideas, and passion.
