What we do

How we support Green ICT & Careful Use Of Internet

Digital Education Programs

We provide comprehensive training and resources to help individuals and communities understand sustainable technology practices and build digital literacy skills aligned with environmental responsibility.

Device Recycling & E-Waste Management

We partner with organizations to properly recycle old electronics and manage e-waste responsibly, preventing harmful materials from entering landfills and ecosystems.

Energy-Efficient Technology Advocacy

We advocate for the adoption of energy-efficient devices and practices, working with businesses and individuals to reduce digital carbon footprints.

Online Privacy & Data Protection

We educate users on protecting their digital privacy, understanding data collection practices, and taking control of their personal information online.
